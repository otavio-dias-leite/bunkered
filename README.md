# bunkered
 A survival bunker simulation game built with Godot.
# About game
World War III has begun. A global nuclear exchange has rendered the Earth's surface uninhabitable, leaving underground, radiation-free bunkers as humanity's last hope.

Design, build, and manage your own bunker. Install vital equipment, expand your underground shelter, and keep your residents alive. Systems will fail, rival bunkers may raid your base, and mutated creatures roaming the wasteland will constantly threaten your survival.

Every resident has unique skills and plays a vital role in your bunker. Recruit specialists such as electricians, hackers, hunters, botanists, investors, and many more to overcome the challenges of life underground.

**Bunkered** is built around realistic scientific and engineering concepts—designed to be authentic without sacrificing fun.

* Design electrical systems by routing live and neutral wiring through your bunker to power equipment while balancing energy generation and consumption.
* Build automation systems to regulate temperature, lighting, water supply, ventilation, and other critical life-support functions.
* Exploit vulnerabilities in rival bunker networks to infiltrate them, while securing your own infrastructure against cyberattacks.
* Repair damaged equipment by diagnosing faults and replacing electronic components on circuit boards.
* Purify contaminated water using realistic filtration systems and chemical treatment processes.

Every system is interconnected. Master engineering, automation, cybersecurity, and resource management to ensure your bunker survives in a world that didn't.
